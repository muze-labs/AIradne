## Plan

### Cycle Goal

Create the initial evidence map for Airadne: extract candidate durable architectural properties from Ariadne and SimplyStore, challenge them with counterexamples, compare competing architecture directions, and identify the cheapest meaningful semantic experiment.

### Commitment Boundary

Human confirmation in conversation on 2026-09-03 after the proposed cycle goal was presented. Upstream accepted request: `REQ-20260903-XCTYG-4`.

### Why Now / Why This Cycle Boundary

The project's next decisions could shape the core semantic model, architecture, or evaluation strategy. A compact evidence map is the smallest useful artifact that can reduce several upstream uncertainties before design begins.

This cycle is intentionally broader than a single Ariadne or SimplyStore reading pass because the comparison between them matters. It is intentionally narrower than architecture design or implementation because the core hypothesis remains unproven.

### Plan Continuity Decision

No governing plan.

This cycle continues the completed greenfield intake and accepted initial request rather than revising or deviating from a roadmap.

### Evaluation Basis

The cycle is successful when it produces a reviewed artifact that:

- separates evidence from interpretation;
- identifies likely durable concepts;
- identifies questionable assumptions and counterexamples;
- compares at least two plausible architecture directions plus one serious non-DSL baseline;
- names falsification targets;
- recommends one small vertical semantic experiment with evaluation criteria;
- does not harden product architecture before evidence supports it.

### Likely Work

- Inspect Ariadne docs/code for long-lived object-space, behavior-selection, authorization, configuration, search/index, and pain-point evidence.
- Inspect SimplyStore docs/code for small-core storage, command/recovery, derived index, query, access, and human-convenience evidence.
- Record initial evidence as a durable `EVD-*` artifact with source references and confidence.
- Synthesize a comparison map and candidate experiment without implementation.

### Explicit Non-Goals

- Do not design a programming language.
- Do not implement product behavior.
- Do not rewrite Ariadne.
- Do not select SimplyStore as the foundation.
- Do not choose a language/runtime/deployment target.
- Do not optimize primarily for LOC.

### Pause / Re-Plan Conditions

- Ariadne or SimplyStore evidence proves too thin without direct human historical input.
- A material contradiction appears in the project frame.
- The cheapest useful next step becomes a human interview or narrower source-gathering pass rather than repository evidence synthesis.
- Evidence suggests the proposed evidence-map boundary cannot fairly compare architecture directions.
